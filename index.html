Sermon Uploader

A small desktop tool for preparing and uploading weekly sermon videos.

Sermon Uploader is a personal macOS app used to process church service recordings and move them to and from the operator's own Google Drive. It:

  • Downloads raw service recordings from a Google Drive folder the operator chooses.
  • Re-encodes the edited sermon video locally on the operator's computer.
  • Uploads the finished files back to the operator's chosen Google Drive folders.

The app runs entirely on the operator's own computer. It signs in with the operator's Google account to access Google Drive, and it does not send data to any server other than Google's.
