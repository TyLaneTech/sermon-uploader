Privacy Policy

Sermon Uploader · Last updated October 11, 2026

Summary

Sermon Uploader is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It uses your Google account only to read and write files in your Google Drive. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

What the app accesses

When you sign in, the app asks Google for permission to access your Google Drive. It uses that access only to:

  • find the Drive folders you choose for recordings and finished videos,
  • list and download recording files from those folders, and
  • upload finished video files to those folders.

The app also reads your Google account email address so it can show which account is signed in.

Where your data goes

All processing happens on your computer. Files move only between your computer and Google Drive. The app has no server of its own and does not send your data, usage information or analytics to the developer or to any third party.

What is stored on your computer

The app saves a Google sign-in token in a file on your computer, readable only by your user account, so you don't have to sign in every time. It also keeps local log files about encoding and upload progress. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Removing access

You can sign out in the app at any time, which deletes the saved token and revokes it with Google. You can also remove the app's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Sermon Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions can be raised by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.
